# Campus Photography Club

A student club for learning photography through short workshops, campus photo walks, and friendly group critiques. Students can take part with a phone or any camera.

## What we do

- Practice composition, lighting, and visual storytelling.
- Share a small selection of photos after each walk and discuss what worked.
- Plan a student photo exhibition at the end of the term.

## How to join

Come to the next club introduction session and bring a phone or camera. New members can start by taking one photo that captures a detail of campus life and sharing it during the group critique.
